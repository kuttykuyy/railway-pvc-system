1. **Time waste** – Hours spent on calculations that don't need expertise
2. **Index hunting** – Searching multiple websites for latest rates
3. **Steel JPC complexity** – 34 items, fortnightly rates, city-wise data
4. **Calculation errors** – Manual formula application leads to mistakes
5. **Record keeping** – Paper files, difficult to retrieve old bills
6. **Staff dependency** – Trained staff leaving = knowledge loss

1. **Automation** – One-click calculations
2. **Auto-fetch** – Direct from PPAC, Labour Bureau, NIC
3. **AI extraction** – Upload PDF, get all 34 items
4. **Built-in formulas** – Railway-compliant, no errors
5. **Digital records** – Search any bill instantly
6. **Standardization** – System knows the process, not just people
